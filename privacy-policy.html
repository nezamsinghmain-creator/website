Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Caribbean Scholars, an online CSEC exam preparation platform operated out of Georgetown, Guyana ("Caribbean Scholars", "we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, and discloses personal information when you visit our website, use our learning platform, or otherwise communicate with us (collectively, the "Services"). "You" and "your" means any user of the Services, including students, parents or guardians, and website visitors.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Services, you agree to the collection, use, and disclosure of information as described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

Caribbean Scholars serves students across the Caribbean, including Guyana, Trinidad and Tobago, Jamaica, and Barbados. This Policy applies to all users regardless of location. Where a user's home country has its own applicable data protection law, that law's requirements apply in addition to, and do not replace, this Policy.

Children's and Minors' Privacy

Caribbean Scholars is a CSEC exam preparation service, and many of our students are minors (typically ages 13–19). We collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide the course and enrollment experience: name, email, and course progress and performance data.

  • Where a student is a minor, we recommend account creation and payment be handled by a parent or guardian, and account communications (renewal notices, progress updates) may be directed to the parent/guardian email on file.
  • We do not knowingly collect more personal information from minors than is necessary to deliver the course, and we do not use minors' data for targeted advertising.
  • A parent or guardian may contact us at any time to review, correct, or request deletion of a minor's information, as described in "Your Rights" below.

How We Collect and Use Your Personal Information

We collect personal information from a variety of sources to operate the Services, communicate with you, process payments, comply with legal obligations, and protect our rights and the rights of our users.

What Personal Information We Collect

  • Information you provide directly: name, address, phone number, and email address, submitted through our enrollment forms or by contacting caribbeanscholars.admin@gmail.com, along with course enrollment and account setup details.
  • Learning platform data: your course progress, quiz and exam results, login activity, and communications within our Moodle-based learning management system.
  • Information collected automatically: usage data regarding your interaction with our site, device details, browser information, IP address, and network connection, collected via cookies and similar technologies.
  • Information from third parties: transaction confirmation data from our regional payment partners (WiPay and Mobile Money Guyana), and data processed through our workflow automation tools (Airtable and Zapier) used to manage enrollments and account setup.

Payment Processing and Financial Data

We use secure regional payment gateways, including Mobile Money Guyana (MMG) and WiPay, to process transactions. These payment processors collect the financial information necessary to complete your transaction (such as mobile wallet identifiers, bank details, or card information) in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable regional financial compliance standards. We do not store sensitive payment credentials, such as full card numbers, on our own servers.

How We Use Your Personal Information

  • Providing the Services: to process payments through MMG or WiPay, enroll you in courses, manage your account, deliver course content through our Moodle platform, and provide progress tracking.
  • Marketing and communication: to send course updates, renewal reminders, and (where you have not opted out) promotional communications by email.
  • Security and fraud prevention: to detect, investigate, and address fraudulent, unauthorized, or malicious activity.
  • Customer support: to respond to inquiries sent to caribbeanscholars.admin@gmail.com.

How We Disclose Personal Information

We share personal information only with trusted service providers who help us operate the Services, including:

  • Moodle hosting and technical service providers, who host and help maintain our learning management system.
  • Airtable and Zapier, which we use to automate enrollment, account setup, and internal record-keeping.
  • Regional payment processors (WiPay and MMG), to facilitate secure transaction processing.
  • Legal or regulatory authorities in Guyana, or in a student's home jurisdiction, when required to comply with applicable law, a subpoena, a court order, or a valid legal request.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share student data with advertisers for third-party marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to meet legal, accounting, or record-keeping obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Course progress and results are generally retained for the duration of your subscription plus 24 months, after which they may be deleted or anonymized unless a longer period is required by law.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including restricted access to account and payment records and secure handling of data by our hosting and payment partners. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, including Guyana's Data Protection Act and relevant regional data protection laws, you (or, for a minor student, their parent or guardian) may:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to our legal and legitimate business retention needs.
  • Opt out of promotional communications at any time, using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us directly.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal or operational reasons, including compliance with Guyana's Data Protection Act. We will post the revised Policy on our site and update the "Last updated" date above.
